Our Story

Handmade Haven began in a reclaimed barn on the outskirts of Asheville, where a group of designers and woodworkers shared a simple belief: beautiful furniture can be made responsibly. Every piece we create is a dialogue between tradition, innovation, and the natural world.

Our Mission

We empower skilled artisans to transform reclaimed timber and responsibly harvested wood into timeless furniture. By sourcing locally, reducing waste, and supporting fair‑pay practices, we aim to inspire a more sustainable home culture.

Maya Rivera – Founder & Creative Director

Maya grew up surrounded by the forests of the Pacific Northwest, learning the art of woodworking from her grandfather. After studying design in Portland, she launched Handmade Haven to merge her love for craftsmanship with a deep commitment to environmental stewardship. Maya leads the creative vision, ensuring each collection tells a story of reclaimed beauty.

Luca Bianchi – Master Woodworker

Born in Tuscany, Luca spent his childhood in his family’s carpentry workshop. He brings centuries‑old joinery techniques to the modern sustainable furniture movement. Luca oversees the transformation of reclaimed timber into heirloom pieces, mentoring the studio’s younger artisans.

Aisha Patel – Sustainability Lead

With a background in environmental science and a passion for circular design, Aisha ensures that every material choice aligns with our low‑impact ethos. She coordinates carbon‑offset projects, partners with reforestation initiatives, and tracks the life‑cycle impact of each product.

Noah Kim – Customer Experience Manager

Noah translates our brand’s values into a seamless shopping experience. He designs our digital touchpoints, curates educational content about furniture care, and builds a community of conscious homeowners who share their own sustainability journeys.

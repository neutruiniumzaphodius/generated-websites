Our Story

From a modest kitchen experiment to a thriving sustainable brand, Sudd’s Paradise has always been driven by a love for clean, ethical suds.

From a Kitchen Table to a Global Community

In 2015, founder Elena Marquez began experimenting with plant‑based oils in her tiny kitchen. Guided by the belief that luxury can be kind, she crafted small batches of vegan, cruelty‑free soaps that quickly won the hearts of friends and family.

When demand grew, Elena moved the process to a dedicated workshop, where she could embrace traditional techniques while innovating with modern, sustainable ingredients. Today, Sudd’s Paradise blends centuries‑old craftsmanship with cutting‑edge eco‑science, delivering a premium experience that never compromises the planet.

Sourcing Practices

All of our raw materials are harvested from regenerative farms that prioritize soil health, biodiversity, and fair‑trade wages. We work directly with growers in the Pacific Northwest, the Mediterranean, and South America, ensuring traceability from seed to suds.

  • 🍃 Organic Coconut Oil – sourced from small‑holder co‑ops in the Philippines.
  • 🌾 Shea Butter – ethically procured from women‑owned cooperatives in Ghana.
  • 🌿 Essential Oils – distilled from sustainably farmed lavender, rosemary, and citrus.

Handcraft Process

Every bar is poured, cut, and cured by hand – a true artisanal ritual.

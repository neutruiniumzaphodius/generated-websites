Our Story

At Pawsitive Reads we believe every child deserves a story that sparks imagination and a learning journey that fuels curiosity.

Our Journey

2015 – The Beginning

Founded in a small Seattle apartment, our co‑founders set out to create a subscription service that brings hand‑picked books to families every month.

2017 – First 1,000 Subscribers

Word spread quickly and we celebrated our first thousand families enjoying curated reading experiences.

2020 – Nationwide Expansion

We partnered with major logistics providers to ship across the United States, reaching more young readers than ever before.

2023 – Community Impact

Our “Books for All” program donated over 10,000 books to schools and libraries in under‑served neighborhoods.

Our Mission

To nurture a lifelong love of reading by delivering age‑appropriate, diverse, and educational books to families across America.

Our Vision

We envision a world where every child has access to stories that reflect their experiences, broaden their horizons, and inspire curiosity.

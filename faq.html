Frequently Asked Questions

How does shipping work?

We ship all orders via standard USPS, UPS or FedEx depending on your location. Shipping is free on all subscriptions within the United States. International shipping rates apply where indicated.

Can I return a book I don’t like?

Absolutely! If a book isn’t a good fit, simply let us know within 14 days of receipt and we’ll arrange a free return or exchange.

How are books selected for my subscription?

Our editorial team curates each title based on age‑appropriate themes, diversity, educational value, and feedback from parents and educators. We aim to inspire a love of reading in every child.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe, as well as PayPal. All transactions are processed securely.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

Yes. You can pause or cancel anytime from your account dashboard. Paused subscriptions will not be billed until you reactivate.

The Moon’s Little Friend

by Jane Doe

← Back to catalog

A gentle picture book about a shy moon and a curious rabbit. When the moon feels lonely and hidden behind clouds, a brave little rabbit hops up the hill and invites the moon to play. Together they discover that even the quietest lights can shine brightest when shared with a friend.

About the Author

Jane Doe is a Seattle‑based children’s author who loves weaving bedtime stories that celebrate wonder and friendship. Her books have been featured in numerous libraries and have won the 2022 Kids’ Choice Storyteller Award.

Policy Details

All orders are processed within 1‑2 business days. Once shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email.

We partner with reliable carriers to ensure safe and timely delivery. Orders may be delayed due to customs, extreme weather, or carrier disruptions; we will keep you informed.

If you need to change or cancel an order after it has been dispatched, please contact us within 24 hours of the shipping confirmation.
